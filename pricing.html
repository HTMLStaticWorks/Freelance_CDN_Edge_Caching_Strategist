Pricing & Engagement FAQ

Clear answers on how I work with your engineering team and handle project scopes.

Do you work with our internal developers/SREs?

Yes, absolutely. I integrate with your existing team workflow. I can write and deliver CDN configuration scripts (such as Terraform, Fastly VCL, or Cloudflare Workers) directly, or work as an SRE specialist to implement them on your staging and production environments.

What platforms do you support?

I have extensive hands-on experience optimizing setups on Cloudflare, Fastly, AWS CloudFront, Akamai, and Vercel Edge. I can tune any third-party CDN you are currently using.

Are there any monthly traffic caps or egress costs?

No. Since I configure your existing CDN accounts directly, your traffic volume billing remains entirely with your CDN provider. My consulting sprints are charged as a single fixed fee with zero hidden usage charges.

How long does an Optimization Sprint take?

A standard optimization sprint takes between 2 to 3 weeks. This includes initial diagnostics, writing custom edge rules, sandbox testing, staged deployment, and validation of the cache efficiency improvements.
