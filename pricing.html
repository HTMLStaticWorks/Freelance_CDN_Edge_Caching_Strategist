Pricing FAQ

Are there setup fees?

No, there are no hidden setup fees for any of our standard plans. Enterprise custom POP setups may involve a one-time configuration fee.

What happens if I exceed my bandwidth?

We don't cut off your traffic. You'll be billed at a flat rate of $0.05 per additional GB on Starter and Pro plans.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. You will retain access to your plan until the end of the current billing cycle.

Do you offer non-profit discounts?

Yes! We offer a 50% discount for registered non-profit organizations. Contact us to learn more.
